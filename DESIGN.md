# Arkodio — Visual Identity & Design System (`DESIGN.md`)

> **Design Read**: Multi-disciplinary creative & tech studio platform for ambitious founders, brands, and young digital creators, in a Warm Amber + Midnight Navy Architectural Frosted Glass & Bento language.
> **Dial**: `ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2`

## 1. Visual Rationale (Written Decisions)
- **Canvas (`#F4F6F9`)**: Soft cool alabaster base provides high optical clarity and 16.49:1 WCAG AAA contrast for Deep Midnight Navy (`#0F172A`) typography while letting translucent frosted layers visibly refract warm amber and deep slate geometric under-blocks.
- **Primary Accent (`#F5A623` -> `#FFB020`)**: Warm Golden Amber anchors ambient under-card light blooms, primary action pills (paired with `#0F172A` text for 8.81:1 contrast), active filter chips, and audio-wave micro-accents.
- **Primary Dark Contrast (`#0F172A` / `#121826`)**: Deep Midnight Navy grounds high-hierarchy bento modules, architectural stat blocks, and the studio footer without making the entire application dark-by-default.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#334155`)**: Frosted Slate serves body copy (9.56:1 contrast) and smoked-glass floating corner badges inspired by the Arkodio brand emblem.
- **Frosted Glass Surfaces**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` specular borders, and `24px` corners establish tactile depth over geometric amber/navy shapes.
- **Typography & Bilingual Parity**: `Space Grotesk` + `Plus Jakarta Sans` for English LTR geometric precision; `Cairo` + `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` for native-grade Arabic RTL readability with zero broken ligatures.
- **Identity Motif**: Bold geometric **Arkodio** wordmark paired with `</>` code brackets and a 5-bar minimal soundwave motif (`Arkodio` = Ark + Code + Audio/Motion).
